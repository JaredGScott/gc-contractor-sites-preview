Custom Homes, Additions & Outbuildings in Williamson County

FIELDHOUSE does three kinds of work on rural land south of Nashville. Here’s what each involves and what’s helpful to have ready.

01 / Custom homes

Custom Homes on Acreage

FIELDHOUSE builds homes on five to fifty acres, planned around the driveway, the septic field, the views and the way you work the land.

Helpful to have ready

Your survey, any soil or septic test results, and the rooms you need.

What we’ll go over

Driveway and site work, septic and water, power to the house, and where the house should sit.

02 / Custom homes

Large Additions

More room for a growing family, a parent or a home office, tied into the original rooflines and built to match.

Helpful to have ready

Photos of the house, any old plans, and why you need more space.

What we’ll go over

Foundations, roof tie-ins, septic capacity for added bedrooms, and how the work affects the house you live in.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Custom homes

Barns, Workshops & Outbuildings

Shops, equipment barns, garages and studios, laid out for the equipment you actually own.

Helpful to have ready

Equipment sizes, how you’ll use the building, and where it will go.

What we’ll go over

Slab and structure, door sizes, power and lighting, ventilation, and the drive and turnaround.
