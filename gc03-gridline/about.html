About us

Meet the GRIDLINE Team

GRIDLINE is a commercial general contractor based in Denver. These are the people who run your job, and this is how we work.

Ray Medina · Owner, GRIDLINE

Your business has to keep running. We plan the job around that.

Most of our clients can’t just close for three months. There are customers coming in, staff who need to work, deliveries at the back door and a landlord with their own rules. Before we price anything, I want to walk the space with whoever runs it day to day, look above the ceiling, and find out what can’t be shut off. Then we write a scope your team can actually read, with every responsibility spelled out.

Who does what

Know who’s accountable for your project.

At the first meeting we’ll name your project manager and superintendent, who submits the permit, which engineers the job needs, and how changes get approved. It all goes in the written scope.

Before you hire a contractor

In Colorado, contractor licensing is handled city by city, so ask for the license in the city where your space is. Then ask who your superintendent will be, and how changes are approved.
