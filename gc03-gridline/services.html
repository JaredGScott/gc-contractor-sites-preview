Services

Tenant Improvements & Commercial Renovations in Metro Denver

GRIDLINE does three kinds of commercial work across metro Denver. Here’s what each one involves and what to have ready for the first meeting.

01 / Commercial

Tenant Improvements

GRIDLINE turns leased shells and second-generation spaces into finished offices, studios and shops: framing and drywall, ceilings, lighting, HVAC and plumbing changes, finishes, and the accessibility work the permit requires.

Helpful to have ready

Your lease work letter, the building’s tenant standards, and any drawings from your architect.

What we’ll go over

What the landlord provides and what you pay for, the building systems we’ll tie into, and how long permits are taking in your city.

Ask about this project↗
02 / Commercial

Commercial Renovations

For spaces that stay open during construction. We phase the work, build temporary walls, schedule noisy work after hours when it’s needed, and keep customers and staff clear of the work area.

Helpful to have ready

Your business hours, the areas that have to stay open, and any drawings of the space.

What we’ll go over

How to phase the job, shutdowns for power or water, after-hours work, and how we keep the work area separate.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Commercial

Medical & Special-Use Buildouts

Clinics, dental offices, salons, fitness studios and other spaces with specific equipment, plumbing and code needs. We bring in the right engineers early so the drawings match what the equipment needs.

Helpful to have ready

Equipment lists and spec sheets, accessibility needs, and design drawings if you have them.

What we’ll go over

Plumbing, power and ventilation for your equipment, any health department requirements, and which consultants the job needs.
