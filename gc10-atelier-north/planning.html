Our approach

How We Plan an Interior Renovation

The finishes are the last step. Here’s how we get there.

01

Look closely

We walk the rooms with you, open up what we can, and check the walls, floors, wiring and plumbing before we plan anything.

02

Work with your designer

If you have an interior designer or architect, we review the drawings early and flag anything that will be hard or costly to build.

03

Settle the details

We work out how cabinets, stone, tile and trim meet, and confirm every dimension before anything is ordered.

04

Build carefully

A project lead is on site every day, the house stays protected and clean, and you see samples and mockups before the final installation.

Common questions

Questions people ask before a renovation

Do you work with interior designers?

Yes, often. Many of our projects are built from a designer’s drawings, and we work closely with them through construction.

Can you help with design if we don’t have a designer?

We can recommend designers we trust, or handle the design of simpler kitchens and baths ourselves.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

Most take a few months from demolition to completion, depending on the size and the lead times for cabinets and stone. We’ll give you a schedule before we start.

Who pulls the permits?

We do, and we schedule the village inspections.

What if we change something during construction?

We write up a change order with the cost and any effect on the schedule, and nothing changes until you sign it.
