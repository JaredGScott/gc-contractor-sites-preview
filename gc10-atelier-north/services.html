Interior, Kitchen & Bath Renovation on the North Shore

Atelier North does three kinds of work on the North Shore. Here’s what each involves and what’s helpful to have ready when we meet.

01 / Renovation

Interior Renovation

Living rooms, libraries and whole floors rebuilt with built-in cabinetry, new lighting and finishes that fit together cleanly.

Helpful to have ready

The rooms you want to change, how you use them, and photos of rooms you like.

What we’ll go over

Existing wiring and plumbing, structural changes, who handles the design, and finish choices.

Ask about this project↗
02 / Renovation

Kitchen & Bath Renovation

Kitchens and bathrooms built from your designer’s drawings or ours, with careful attention to cabinets, tile, stone and ventilation.

Helpful to have ready

Design drawings if you have them, appliance and fixture choices, and how you use the room.

What we’ll go over

Dimensions, plumbing and electrical locations, waterproofing, ventilation, and how the materials meet.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Renovation

Small Additions

Garden rooms, mudrooms and primary suites, detailed to match the house inside and out.

Helpful to have ready

What the new room is for and the existing details you want to match.

What we’ll go over

Structure, light, how the new room connects to the old, and matching interior finishes.
