Services

Remodeling, Additions & General Construction

Most of what GOODSTEAD builds falls into three kinds of work. Here’s what each one involves and what’s helpful to have ready when we come out.

01 / General contracting

Kitchen & Bathroom Remodeling

Most of our remodeling work is kitchens and bathrooms, along with basements, laundry rooms and the rooms in between. GOODSTEAD handles the carpentry, coordinates the plumbing and electrical, and talks through how you’ll live around the work before anything starts.

Helpful to have ready

Photos of the room, what bugs you about it, and anything you’re keeping (like the fridge you just bought).

What we’ll go over

What could be hiding behind the walls, which rooms you’ll lose for a while, and what’s in the price versus what you’ll pick out.

02 / General contracting

Home Additions & Garage Conversions

For when the house is simply too small: a family room off the kitchen, a primary suite, a second-story addition, or turning an underused garage into a real room.

Helpful to have ready

What the new space is for, a survey or plot plan if you have one, and any old drawings of the house.

What we’ll go over

Where an addition can go on your lot, what the foundation and roof tie-in involve, the permits it needs, and how we get equipment into the yard.

Ask about this project↗
03 / General contracting

General Construction

Bigger projects with a lot of trades: framing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, insulation, drywall and finish carpentry. We schedule the trades, check their work, and you deal with one person instead of six.

Helpful to have ready

Drawings if you have them, your budget range, and when you’d like to be finished.

What we’ll go over

Who does what, which trades are our crew and which are subcontractors, and how changes get priced and signed off.
