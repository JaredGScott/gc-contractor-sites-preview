The project

What wasn’t working

The family had one usable stretch of counter. Everything else was covered in appliances and mail, and the kids cut through the cooking area on the way to the back door.

What we did

We moved the refrigerator to the end wall, built a seven-foot island with drawers instead of doors, added lights under the cabinets, and kept the walkway from the back door to the hall clear.
