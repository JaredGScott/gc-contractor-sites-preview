Kitchen, Bathroom & Whole-Home Remodeling in the Twin Cities

Common Ground does three kinds of remodeling in the Twin Cities. Here’s what each one involves and what’s helpful to have ready when we come out.

01 / Remodeling

Kitchen Remodeling

Most of the kitchens we remodel are in houses built between the 1940s and the 1970s: small, closed off and short on counter space. Common Ground reworks the layout, opens walls where it makes sense, and plans the cabinets around what you actually cook and store.

Helpful to have ready

Photos of the kitchen, what bugs you about it, and any appliances you’re keeping.

What we’ll go over

Layout options, which walls can move, plumbing and electrical changes, and where you’ll cook while the kitchen is torn out.

02 / Remodeling

Bathroom Remodeling

From a tired hall bath the whole family shares to a new primary bathroom. We fix what’s behind the walls, like venting, old plumbing and soft subfloors, and build a room that’s easier to use and easier to clean.

Helpful to have ready

Who uses the room, anything that makes it hard to use, and a few photos.

What we’ll go over

Tub or shower, storage, ventilation, what we might find behind the tile, and how long you’ll be without the room.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Remodeling

Whole-Home Remodeling

When the problem isn’t one room but how the rooms connect. We plan several rooms together, widen openings, carry one floor through, and phase the work so you can stay in the house.

Helpful to have ready

A list of the rooms, what matters most, and whether you plan to live at home during the work.

What we’ll go over

Which walls are load-bearing, the order the work happens in, and which parts of the house you’ll have during each phase.
