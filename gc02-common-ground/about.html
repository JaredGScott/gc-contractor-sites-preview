Meet the Common Ground Team

Common Ground is a local remodeling company. These are the people who’ll be in your house, and this is how we run a job.

Alex Morgan · Owner, Common Ground

You’re going to live with us for a while. We take that seriously.

When we remodel your kitchen, you’re living with us for a while: a crew in the house most weekdays, dust walls up, and dinner on a hot plate in the dining room. So before we talk about cabinets, I want to know how your household runs. Who leaves first in the morning, where the dog goes, whether someone works from home. Then we plan the job around that and tell you plainly which rooms you’ll lose and for how long.

Who does what

Know who’s running your remodel.

On the first visit we’ll tell you who will run your remodel day to day, who pulls the permits, and how changes get written up and approved. All of it goes in your written proposal.

Before you hire a remodeler

Minnesota licenses residential remodelers through the Department of Labor and Industry, so ask for the license number and look it up. Then ask who will actually be in your house, and how changes are handled.
