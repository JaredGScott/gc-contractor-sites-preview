The look for every photo on this site

Remodeled family homes: white walls with blue-gray and soft cobalt cabinetry, natural oak, white quartz, matte black or brushed nickel hardware. Professional remodeling-portfolio photography, exactly like the project photos on the websites of top design-build firms (Airoom, BOWA, Case Architects & Remodelers, Normandy Remodeling, Neil Kelly). Bright, even natural daylight with the room's own lights switched on; balanced exposure so windows show the trees and sky outside instead of glowing white; true, clean colors; straight vertical lines; wide but not distorted; sharp from front to back. Light, believable styling: fresh flowers, a bowl of lemons or green apples, a few cookbooks, a folded towel, never cluttered. Upscale but real materials with real texture: wood grain, stone veining, tile grout lines, brushed brass. Exteriors on a clear day, or at twilight with every interior light on and the sky still blue, with neat lawn and planting.
