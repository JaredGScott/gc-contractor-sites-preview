About the project

What wasn’t working

The owners wanted to cook and still keep an eye on homework, but a small peninsula and a wall cut the kitchen off from everything else.

What we changed

We took out the peninsula, built a long oak island with seating, added a floor-to-ceiling pantry wall, put lights under every cabinet, and kept a clear path from the hall to the backyard door.
