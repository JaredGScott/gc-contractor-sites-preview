About the project

What wasn’t working

One small vanity, one dim light and no fan made the only full bathroom the most stressful room in the house.

What we changed

We fit a double vanity in the same footprint, recessed two medicine cabinets into the wall, added a quiet exhaust fan, and put in a tub-shower with a hand shower for bathing the kids.
