About us

Meet the Stillwater Team

Stillwater is a small, family-owned builder in Gig Harbor. When you build with us, these are the people you’ll see on your site.

Daniel Brooks · Owner, Stillwater

Every lot out here is different. That’s where we start.

Most of the homes we build sit on lots with big firs, a slope, a view of the water, or all three. Before anyone draws a floor plan, I want to walk the property with you: where the sun comes in, where the water goes when it rains, which trees you want to keep, and how a concrete truck gets down the driveway. Then we talk about how you want to live in the house. You’ll know what the site is going to take before you commit to anything.

Daniel Brooks

Our crew

Our own carpenters frame and finish our homes.

Stillwater carpenters frame, side and trim the houses we build. Excavation, concrete, plumbing, electrical and HVAC go to subcontractors we’ve worked with for years, and your project manager is the one person you call with questions.

Who does what

Know who’s building your home.

On the first visit we’ll tell you who will run your project day to day, who submits the permits, which engineers and consultants your lot is likely to need, and how changes get approved. All of it goes in your written proposal.

Before you hire a builder

Check that the builder is registered as a contractor in Washington, ask who will actually run your job, and ask how changes and allowances are handled. A good builder will answer all three without hesitating.
