Services

Custom Homes, Additions & Site Planning in Kitsap County

Stillwater does three kinds of work, all of it on the Kitsap Peninsula. Here’s what each one involves and what’s helpful to have ready when we meet.

01 / Custom homes

Custom Home Building

Stillwater builds one-of-a-kind homes on your land, from a single-level house on a flat lot to a three-story home stepped down a waterfront bluff. We work with your architect, or introduce you to one we trust, and manage the trades from the foundation to the final walkthrough.

Helpful to have ready

Your survey or plot plan, any drawings or sketches, and photos of homes you like.

What we’ll go over

Well or water hookup, septic or sewer, power to the site, tree removal, driveway access, and what it will take to get the lot ready before the house goes up.

02 / Custom homes

Home Additions

For when the house is in the right place but too small. We build additions that match the original roofline, siding and floor heights, so the new rooms feel like they were always there.

Helpful to have ready

Photos of the house, the original drawings if you have them, and what the new space needs to do.

What we’ll go over

How the new roof and foundation tie into the old ones, which rooms you’ll lose during the work, and the permits involved.

Ask about this service↗
03 / Custom homes

Preconstruction & Site Planning

Most of the surprises on a custom home are in the ground. Before construction we look at soils, drainage, slopes, septic design and utility routes, and help you line up the studies and permits in the right order.

Helpful to have ready

The lot address or parcel number, any studies already done, and your target budget.

What we’ll go over

Which reports the county will ask for, how long permits are taking right now, and which design choices move the price the most.
