Home Additions & Conversions in Cary & the Triangle

room to grow does three kinds of work around Cary and Raleigh. Here’s what each involves and what’s helpful to have ready when we come out.

01 / Additions

Family Room Additions

A bigger place to gather, usually off the kitchen and opening to the yard. We tie the new roof and floor into the house so the room feels like it was always there.

Helpful to have ready

How your family uses the house now, and what feels crowded.

What we’ll go over

Where the addition can go on your lot, setbacks and HOA rules, and how it changes the rooms next to it.

02 / Additions

Bedroom & In-Law Suites

A private suite for a parent, a teenager or guests, with its own bathroom and, when it’s needed, a step-free entry and shower.

Helpful to have ready

Who will use the suite, their privacy needs, and any mobility needs.

What we’ll go over

Layout, plumbing and HVAC for the new space, step-free design, and permits.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Additions

Garage & Attic Conversions

Turning space you already have into a real room. It’s often the most affordable way to add square footage.

Helpful to have ready

Photos of the garage or attic, any plans of the house, and what you want the room to do.

What we’ll go over

Headroom, the slab or floor framing, insulation, heating and cooling, stairs, and what the code requires for a bedroom.
