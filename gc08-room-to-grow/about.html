About us

Meet the room to grow Team

room to grow is a small, local company that builds additions and conversions. These are the people who’ll be at your house.

Robin Clark · Owner, room to grow

Moving isn’t the only way to get more room.

Most families who call us have already looked at houses for sale and decided they’d rather stay: the school is right, the neighbors are good, the yard is finally how they want it. So we start with why you need the space. A parent moving in needs something different from a home office or a playroom. Then we look at the lot, the house and the budget, and tell you plainly whether to build out, build up or convert space you already have.

Who does what

Know who’s building your addition.

On the first visit we’ll tell you who will run your project, who draws the plans, who pulls the permits, and how changes are approved. All of it goes in your written proposal.

Before you hire a contractor

North Carolina requires a licensed general contractor for most projects over $40,000, and you can look up any license on the state licensing board’s website. Ask who will actually be on site, and how changes are handled.
