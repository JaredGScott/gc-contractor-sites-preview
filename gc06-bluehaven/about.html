About us

Meet the Bluehaven Team

Bluehaven is a small, local builder on Cape Cod. These are the people who will build your home.

Casey Bennett · Owner, Bluehaven

A house near the water has to work hard.

People come to us for the view, but the view is the easy part. A house a few hundred yards from the ocean takes salt spray, nor’easters, sandy feet and wet dogs. Before we talk about windows, I want to know how you’ll use the place: where you come in from the beach, where the outdoor shower goes, which rooms stay closed up all winter. Then we pick materials and details that will still look good in twenty years.

Casey Bennett

Our crew

Our own carpenters frame, shingle and trim.

Bluehaven carpenters handle the framing, shingles, trim and decks. Plumbing, electrical and HVAC go to licensed subcontractors we’ve worked with for years, and your project manager is the one person you call.

We build for the weather
Materials and details chosen for salt air, wind and sand.
We plan for life outside
Porches, outdoor showers and mudrooms get as much thought as the kitchen.
Changes go in writing
Every change is priced and signed before we do it.
Building on the coast↗

Who does what

Know who’s building your home.

On the first visit we’ll tell you who will run your project, who handles the permits and town approvals, which engineers and surveyors you’ll need, and how changes are approved. All of it goes in your written proposal.

Before you hire a builder

Massachusetts requires a Construction Supervisor License for most building work, and home improvement contractors must also be registered with the state. Ask for both numbers and look them up.
