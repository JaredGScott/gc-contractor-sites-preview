Coastal Custom Homes, Renovations & Additions on Cape Cod

Bluehaven does three kinds of work on Cape Cod. Here’s what each involves and what’s helpful to have ready when we meet.

01 / Custom homes

Coastal Custom Homes

New homes on Cape Cod lots, from cottages to year-round family houses. Bluehaven places the house for the views and the weather, and builds with materials chosen for salt air and wind.

Helpful to have ready

Your lot survey or plot plan, any flood zone or conservation information, and how you’ll use the house.

What we’ll go over

Flood zone and conservation commission requirements, the septic system, wind and salt exposure, and outdoor living space.

02 / Custom homes

Indoor-Outdoor Renovations

Opening your house to the porch, deck and yard with wider glass doors, covered outdoor rooms, outdoor showers and mudrooms that catch the sand.

Helpful to have ready

Photos of the rooms and the parts of the yard you use most.

What we’ll go over

Structural openings, drainage, door and window choices for coastal exposure, and shade.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Custom homes

Home Additions

Guest wings, primary suites and family rooms that match the original shingles and trim and stay within your lot’s limits.

Helpful to have ready

What the new space is for, any existing plans, and the parts of the house you want to keep.

What we’ll go over

Setbacks and zoning, roof tie-ins, exterior materials, and town approvals.
