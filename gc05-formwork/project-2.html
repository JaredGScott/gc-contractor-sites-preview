What the owners wanted

The narrow lot faced a busy street, and the owners wanted daylight without living in a fishbowl.

How we designed and built it

We closed the street side with a solid wall and high windows, opened every main room to a central courtyard with floor-to-ceiling glass, and shaded the west glass with a deep roof overhang.
