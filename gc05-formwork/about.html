About us

Meet the FORMWORK Team

FORMWORK is a design-build firm in Austin. Our designers and builders work as one team, and these are the people you’ll work with.

Taylor Reed · Founder, FORMWORK

Design and construction belong in the same conversation.

Too many people fall in love with a set of drawings and only then find out what it costs to build them. We do it the other way around. I sit down with you and one of our builders on the first day, and we price the big ideas while they’re still sketches: the wall that comes out, the glass that faces west, the concrete floor. You decide with real numbers in front of you, and the house you get is the house you designed.

Taylor Reed

Our crew

The people who draw it help build it.

FORMWORK designers stay on your project through construction, and our own carpenters handle framing, cabinetry and finish work. Plumbing, electrical and HVAC go to licensed subcontractors we’ve worked with for years.

Who does what

Know who’s designing and building your project.

At the first meeting we’ll tell you who designs your project, who manages construction, who handles permits and engineering, and how changes are approved. All of it goes in your design agreement and construction contract.

Before you hire a builder

Texas doesn’t license general contractors statewide, so ask for proof of insurance and references, and make sure the plumbers, electricians and HVAC techs on your job hold their state licenses.
