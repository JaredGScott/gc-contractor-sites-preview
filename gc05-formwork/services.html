Services

Design-Build, Modern Renovations & Custom Homes in Austin

FORMWORK does three kinds of work in Austin, all of it designed and built by one team. Here’s what each involves.

01 / Design-build

Design-Build

FORMWORK designs and builds under one contract. Our designers draw the project while our builders price it, so the design never gets ahead of the budget.

Helpful to have ready

What you want to change, photos or a survey of the house, and a budget range.

What we’ll go over

Design fees, how the design and pricing phases work, and the decisions that drive the cost the most.

Ask about this project↗
02 / Design-build

Modern Renovations

Opening up closed floor plans, bringing in light, and rebuilding kitchens and baths in Austin’s older houses, with built-in storage and simple, durable materials.

Helpful to have ready

How the house works now and the rooms you want to use differently.

What we’ll go over

Which walls can come out, structural and foundation work, and what the renovation means for the rest of the house.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Design-build

Custom Homes

New modern homes designed around the lot, the sun and the heat, from small infill houses to family homes on larger lots.

Helpful to have ready

Your lot survey, any deed restrictions or HOA rules, and the rooms you need.

What we’ll go over

Setbacks and impervious cover, the foundation, orientation for shade, and the systems that keep a house cool.
