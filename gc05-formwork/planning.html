How we work

How Design-Build Works at FORMWORK

One team designs and builds your project. Here’s how the work moves from the first sketch to move-in.

01

First meeting

We meet at your house with a designer and a builder, talk through what you want, and look at the structure, foundation and systems.

02

Design and price together

Our designers draw options while our builders price them. You choose with real numbers in front of you.

03

Final drawings and a written price

Once the design is set, we finish the construction drawings and give you a detailed written price and schedule.

04

Build

The same team builds what it designed. You’ll have one project manager, a written update every week and a regular site walk.

Common questions

Questions people ask about design-build

What is design-build?

One company designs and builds your project under one contract. You work with one team from the first sketch to the final walkthrough, instead of hiring an architect and a builder separately.

How does the design fee work?

Design is a separate first agreement. It covers measuring the house, design options, pricing and construction drawings, and you’ll know what it costs before it starts.

Can you work with our architect?

Yes. If you already have drawings, we can build from them or help adjust them to fit your budget.

Who handles permits?

We do. We submit to the City of Austin or your county and handle the inspections.

What if we change our minds during construction?

We write up a change order with the cost and any effect on the schedule, and nothing changes until you sign it.
