About the project

What the owners wanted

The kitchen had been remodeled badly in the 1980s and had almost no counter or storage space.

What we kept and what we changed

We took out the dropped ceiling and the 1980s cabinets, built painted cabinets and a pantry up to the ceiling, added an island sized for the room, and kept the original wood window and trim over the sink.
