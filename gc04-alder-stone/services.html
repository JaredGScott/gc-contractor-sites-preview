Older-Home Renovation, Kitchens & Additions in Richmond

Alder & Stone works on older Richmond homes, most of them built between 1900 and 1945. Here’s what each kind of project involves.

01 / Renovation

Whole-Home Renovation

Bringing an older house up to date without losing what makes it special. We update the layout, wiring, plumbing and insulation, and repair or match the original trim, floors and plaster wherever we can.

Helpful to have ready

The parts of the house you love, the parts that don’t work, and any old drawings or inspection reports.

What we’ll go over

What can be saved, what has to be replaced, what we won’t know until the walls are open, and how the work can be phased.

02 / Renovation

Kitchen Renovation

A kitchen that works for the way you cook now but still looks like it belongs in the house: painted cabinets, a sensible island, and the original windows and trim kept or carefully matched.

Helpful to have ready

How you cook and who’s in the kitchen, plus the original details you want to keep.

What we’ll go over

Layout, cabinets, where the appliances go, old plumbing and wiring, and how we’ll match the existing trim.

Ask about this project↗
03 / Renovation

Additions to Older Homes

More room without spoiling the house from the street. We keep additions in proportion, use a lower roofline where it helps, and take care with the spot where old meets new.

Helpful to have ready

Why you need the space, photos of the outside of the house, and any earlier additions or changes.

What we’ll go over

Where the addition can go, how it ties into the old foundation and roof, materials that suit the house, and the permits, including historic district review if your street has one.
