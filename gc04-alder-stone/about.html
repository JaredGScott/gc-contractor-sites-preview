About us

Meet the Alder & Stone Team

Alder & Stone is a small renovation company that works only on older homes. These are the people who’ll be in your house.

Jamie Porter · Owner, Alder & Stone

We look closely at a house before we change it.

Older houses tell you a lot if you take the time. The stair rail worn smooth, the heart pine under the carpet, the window someone painted shut in 1970. Before we draw anything, I walk every room with you, go up in the attic and down in the basement, and make a list of what’s worth keeping. Then we talk honestly about the wiring, the plumbing and the framing, because in a hundred-year-old house that’s where the surprises are.

Jamie Porter

Our crew

Carpenters who know old houses.

Alder & Stone carpenters do the framing, trim, doors and built-ins themselves, including fitting new trim to match the original. Electricians, plumbers and plasterers we’ve worked with for years handle their parts.

Who does what

Know who’s working on your house.

On the first visit we’ll tell you who runs your renovation day to day, who pulls the permits, whether you need an architect or engineer, and how changes are approved. All of it goes in the written proposal.

Before you hire a contractor

Virginia contractors are licensed by the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation, and you can look up any license online. Ask who will actually be in your house, and how changes are handled.
