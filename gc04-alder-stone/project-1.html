About the project

What the owners wanted

The owners loved the formal front rooms but had a cramped kitchen and no family room facing the yard.

What we kept and what we changed

We kept and repaired the original trim, doors and heart pine floors in the front rooms, then built a garden-facing extension with tall glass doors. The wide opening between old and new is cased to match the 1920s trim.
